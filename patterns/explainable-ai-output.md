# Explainable AI Output

## Problem

AI-generated classifications, scores, or insights without explanation create false confidence in investigators and are challengeable in legal contexts. A reviewer cannot evaluate an output they cannot trace. An AI score with no source citation, confidence explanation, or link to underlying evidence is not a finding; it is a number without context. In court, it is a liability.

## Design Guidance

Every AI output must cite the source evidence it is based on. Confidence indicators should be expressed in plain language alongside any numerical score. Explain how the model reached a conclusion in terms a non-technical reviewer can evaluate.

Label all AI-generated content distinctly from human-reviewed content. The distinction should be visible at the point of use, not only in documentation.

Human-in-the-loop checkpoints are required for high-impact AI recommendations. The system should not take irreversible action based on an AI output without a human confirmation step.

## Do

- "Flagged as CSAM by perceptual hash classifier (high confidence, 0.94). Based on 3 matched hashes. Review source artifacts: [link to artifacts]."
- AI badge on all AI-generated classifications, distinct from human-reviewed labels
- "This classification was generated by AI. A human reviewer has not verified this finding."
- Confidence in plain language: "High confidence," "Low confidence," with a tooltip explaining what that means for this model

## Don't

- "AI score: 0.94" with no explanation of what the score means or what it was based on
- AI-generated and human-reviewed content displayed with identical visual treatment
- AI recommendations that trigger irreversible actions without a human confirmation step
- Confidence percentages without calibration context (what does 0.94 mean for this model's false positive rate?)

## Risk Level

**High** - Failure produces unexplainable outputs that cannot be defended in legal proceedings and may cause investigators to act on unverified findings.

## Principles

- Trustworthiness and Transparency
- Safety
